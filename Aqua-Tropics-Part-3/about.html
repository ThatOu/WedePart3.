About Aqua Tropics

Founded in 2020 in Durban, Aqua Tropics has grown from a passionate hobbyist garage project into KwaZulu-Natal's leading retail center for freshwater aquatic life.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable fishkeeping by educating enthusiasts, providing healthy specimens, and offering eco-friendly aquarium technology.

Our Vision

To lead South Africa's aquatic industry in biosecurity standards, captive breeding advocacy, and community outreach.
